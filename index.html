Our Heritage

Rooted in Tradition,
Crafted with Love

For over six decades, Maruthi Divine Touch has been the guardian of India's incense-making heritage. Our master artisans hand-roll each agarbatti using time-honored techniques passed down through generations.

We source the finest natural ingredients — sandalwood from Karnataka, jasmine from Tamil Nadu, and rare resins from the forests of Assam. Every fragrance tells a story of devotion and craftsmanship.
